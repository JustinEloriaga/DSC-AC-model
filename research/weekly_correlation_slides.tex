\begin{frame}{The joint Bayesian target}
\begin{align*}
y_t&=B_t+\varepsilon_t,\qquad\varepsilon_t\sim N(0,D_tP_tD_t),\\
r_t&=\operatorname{vecl}(\log P_t),\qquad r_t=r_{t-1}+v_t.
\end{align*}
\begin{itemize}\item 14 time-varying means and 14 log variances.\item 91 correlation coordinates produce positive-definite matrices.\item Historical smoothing uses the entire sample.\item $p=0$ baseline. Serial dependence remains a production design issue.\end{itemize}
\vfill\tiny Archakov and Hansen (2021). Actual entries of $P_t$, rather than matrix-log coordinates, are the reported correlations.
\end{frame}

\appendix
\begin{frame}{Technical appendix: priors and storage}
\small
First 104 returns calibrate initial priors, with 5\% covariance shrinkage. All observations remain in the likelihood.\par\medskip
The 104-week rolling calibration sets inverse-gamma innovation priors with shape 10. Sensitivity windows are 52 and 156 weeks. Full-sample calibration reuses data.\par\medskip
Cumulative-normal random-walk draws replace dense temporal factors exactly. Checkpoints preserve chain identity, RNG state, dates and the source hash.\par\medskip
MAT-v7.3 chunks store distinct correlation entries. Per-chain burn-in exclusion occurs before summaries.
\end{frame}
